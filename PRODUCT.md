# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Developers and tech workers browsing Hacker News during office hours on desktop monitors in bright ambient light. They scan headlines, read articles and comments, and follow threaded discussions. The primary task is consuming and navigating dense text content, often in long sessions.

## Product Purpose

A Chrome extension that improves Hacker News readability. It exists because HN's default styling is cramped, hierarchy is flat, and threaded comment discussions are hard to follow visually. Success means the reader forgets about the styling entirely and focuses on content.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, focused, transparent. The extension should feel like HN always should have looked, not like a redesign. Understated confidence — good typography and spacing speak for themselves.

## Anti-references

- No flashy redesigns that scream "browser extension"
- No dark-mode optimization (daytime office use)
- No decorative elements, gradients, or glassmorphism
- No fixed-width narrow columns that waste monitor space
- No Reddit-style comment folding or social-media visual clutter

## Design Principles

1. **Clear hierarchy** — Thread depth, parent/child relationships in comments must be instantly recognizable without squinting
2. **Comfortable reading** — Type size, line height, and measure tuned for long-form text at desk distance
3. **Preserve familiarity** — HN's orange accent and structural layout remain recognizable; improve, don't replace
4. **Minimal visual noise** — Every color, rule, and spacing value earns its place; nothing decorative

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No specific WCAG target. Respect system font-size preferences. Ensure sufficient contrast for office-lit screens. Animations limited to opacity transitions.
