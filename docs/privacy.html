Privacy

Style for Hacker News keeps your reading choices in your browser.

What it does

The extension improves the presentation of Hacker News and remembers the reading choices you make, such as typeface and how much story information to show.

How information is handled

Your choices stay in your browser. The extension does not collect, sell, transmit or share personal information, browsing history, page content or usage data.

Independence and support

This is an independent project, not affiliated with or endorsed by Y Combinator or Hacker News. Report privacy or security concerns through the project support page.
