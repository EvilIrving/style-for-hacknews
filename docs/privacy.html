Privacy

Style for Hacker News works locally in your browser.

Where it runs

The extension runs only on https://news.ycombinator.com/*, where it injects local styles and its reading controls.

Data handling

The extension stores your font and metadata-visibility choices with the browser’s storage API. It does not collect, sell, transmit or share personal information, browsing history, page content or usage analytics.

Permissions

The site permission is needed to apply the selected typography and layout to Hacker News pages. The storage permission keeps your local choices between visits.

Independence and support

This is an independent project, not affiliated with or endorsed by Y Combinator or Hacker News. Report privacy or security concerns through the project issue tracker.
